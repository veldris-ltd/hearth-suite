# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in the Hearth Suite
community a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless
of background or identity. We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standard

We adopt the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1**, as the standard of conduct for this
project. Please read the full text, including the expected behaviour, enforcement
responsibilities, scope, and enforcement guidelines:

> https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/

In short: be kind and considerate, assume good faith, give and accept constructive feedback
gracefully, and respect differing viewpoints. Behaviour that makes others feel unwelcome or
unsafe is not acceptable.

## Scope

This Code applies within all project spaces, the repository, issues, pull requests,
discussions, and any other official channel, and whenever an individual is representing the
project in public spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Concerns may be reported privately to the project maintainers at
**hello@veldris.io**. All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and
fairly, and reporters' privacy and security will be respected.

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code and may take any action
they deem appropriate, up to and including a temporary or permanent ban from the community,
following the enforcement guidelines in the Contributor Covenant linked above.
